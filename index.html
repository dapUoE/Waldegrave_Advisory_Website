About Waldegrave Advisory

Your Trusted Partner in Financial Risk Management

At Waldegrave Advisory, we bring decades of experience from global financial markets to provide bespoke risk management solutions for businesses of all sizes.

Alexander Price

Founder & Managing Director

With over 25 years of experience in international financial markets, Alexander founded Waldegrave Advisory to bring institutional-grade risk management expertise to a broader range of clients.

Our team combines deep market knowledge with advanced analytical tools to help you navigate the complexities of financial risk across foreign exchange, commodities, and cryptocurrency markets.

We believe in building long-term relationships with our clients, becoming an extension of your team to develop and implement risk management strategies that align with your business objectives.

Alexander has extensive experience working at Citi Bank, HSBC, Deutche Bank, and RBC, in the European FX Sales team, where he provided best-in-class risk advisory solutions to SME large corporate clients and financial institutions across Europe and North America.
